Sindhudurg

Kokum trees grow along the red laterite coast. Picked by hand in April and May, when the fruit turns deep purple.

The rind

Only the rind goes in the can. The seeds go back to the farmers, who press them for kokum butter.

7 days of sun

Rinds dry on cloth for a week, the way Konkani kitchens have always done it.
